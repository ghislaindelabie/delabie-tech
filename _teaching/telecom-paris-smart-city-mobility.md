A recurring executive-education programme at Télécom Paris that examines smart mobility from technology, business-model, and stakeholder perspectives. I have taught modules in this programme since 2021 — five years and counting.

The programme is co-led by **Gabriel Plassat** (Engineer at ADEME, director of La Fabrique des Mobilités, EU Commission expert, ANSES advisor) and runs at €2,180 for 2026, 2.5 days in-person in Paris, with distance + English options available.

## Curriculum (captured 2026-05-19)

### Introduction — The city as a new living laboratory

- Global context: constraints and opportunities for living together better
- Stakeholder panorama: local authorities, global/national enterprises, entrepreneurs, labs, schools
- Smart City definitions
- City business models

### The State facing smart mobility — public digital infrastructure

- Etalab, État-plateforme, Open Data and public APIs
- Public digital infrastructures: transport.data.gouv.fr, le.taxi, the carpooling proof registry
- Connected and sustainable mobility

### Behind the flows — citizens

- The MaaS ecosystem and its data
- Producers, consumers, reusers, intermediaries
- The role of citizens in co-constructing data, maps, IT systems
- Curb digitalisation (US/Canadian cities, including Montréal)
- Significance, objectives, inventory, policy understanding/improvement, monetisation

### How to co-design the next mobility services in Smart Cities

- Existing innovation-support mechanisms — FabMob.io
- Project presentations: toward the city platform
- The key role of actors including start-ups — entrepreneur innovation, production, global diffusion

### Synthesis and conclusion

## Target audience (as captured)

- Technical leadership, transport engineering departments, manufacturers / constructors
- Mobility authorities
- Elected officials, leaders, territorial collective managers
- Transport operators
- Marketing / communication directions for mobility and transport
- Telecommunications operators
- Road / rail infrastructure managers, multimodal exchange poles, parking managers
- IT services, engineers
- Urbanists, architects

Familiarity with the web and transport sectors is recommended but not required.
