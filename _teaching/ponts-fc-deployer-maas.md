A three-day executive education programme at École des Ponts ParisTech (Ponts Formation Continue), addressing the operational deployment of Mobility-as-a-Service (MaaS) systems by French Authorities Organising Mobility (AOM) under the LOM legislation.

I **designed the programme** and lead its delivery. A second programme on **AI + mobility** is in development with Ponts Formation Continue along the same model.

The programme is taught in person in Paris over 21 hours (three days), priced at €2,630 excl. tax. No prerequisites required.

## Pedagogical objectives (as captured 2026-05-19)

- **DEFINE** a MaaS project in a complex stakeholder environment.
- **APPROPRIATE** the methods for MaaS architecture and design through a red-thread case study.
- **IMPLEMENT** and run a MaaS project, adopting the governance, cooperation modes and contractual strategy that unite internal and external partners across a long-term project.

## Target audience

- Local authorities and AOMs
- Engineering and advisory bureaus
- Digital technology companies
- Operators and exploitants
- Deconcentrated State services
- Mobility and MaaS operators

## Pedagogical methods

- Self-positioning questionnaire (prerequisites, experience, expectations)
- Theoretical and methodological inputs
- Concrete illustrations and application examples
- Case study analysis
- Quizzes
- Experience feedback
- Evaluation through application examples, case study and quizzes
